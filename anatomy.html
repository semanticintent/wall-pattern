Parts of a block

  1. 1
    Step & name
    Numbered, so the order reads at a glance from any distance.
  2. 2
    State chip
    Calm, Watch or Act. Grey by default; colour and the glowing frame only when the step needs eyes.
  3. 3
    Hero number
    The one figure that answers “is this step OK?”
  4. 4
    Delta badge
    ↑/↓ coloured by meaning, not direction: blue is better, orange is worse. Here fewer approvals is worse. Pulses once on change, then fades.
  5. 5
    Indicator rows
    Supporting counts, each with its own threshold dot.
  6. 6
    Age vs target
    How long the oldest item has waited, against the target.
  7. 7
    Exception badge
    Exceptions show where they happen, not only in their own panel.

Rules for the wall

Readable from four metres. Big numbers, few words. Grey is normal. Colour means “look here”. Draw the flow. Connectors carry counts, stay quiet when nothing moved, and turn amber when a handoff stalls. Show it’s alive. A heartbeat and an “updated” time; a missing feed shows Stale. Fix the Δ baseline. Since the last update, or since the start of the shift, day or week. Gentle motion. One pulse per change; reduced-motion settings are respected.
